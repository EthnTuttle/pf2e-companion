# Style block — reformed (woodcut / engraving, symbols over persons)

Register (grounded hard in `reformed/deities/yhwh.md`: "worshipped without
images (the regulative principle)… sacred colors are black and white";
Calvin, Institutes I.11-12; Heidelberg Catechism LD 35 Q.96-98): the
16th-century print — Geneva Bible woodcut, pamphlet engraving, later
steel-engraved frontispiece. Black line on cream paper, cross-hatching, flat
mid-grey tone.

CRITICAL SENSITIVITY: the Second Commandment is load-bearing (Westminster LC
109 forbids images of any Person of the Godhead "inwardly in our mind, or
outwardly in any kind of image"). This pack's visual *theology* is its
austerity — the restraint is the flavor, not a limitation.

Palette (named colors): iron-gall ink black, cream laid paper, one flat
mid-grey tone, at most one warm india-ink wash.

## Prefix

A Reformation-era woodcut engraving, black ink on cream laid paper, dense
cross-hatching, stark black-and-white with one warm india-ink wash at most,
quiet symbolic still-life or narrative scene composition, no devotional
portrait framing, no divine person depicted, no halos. Subject:

## Suffix

Reverent devotional-fantasy illustration, dignified, no gore. Absolutely no
text, no letters, no inscriptions (woodcut word-blocks must remain abstract
pen-strokes), no watermark, no border, no frame, no logo. No copyrighted
fantasy-franchise visual references. No real identifiable living or recent
persons. No Person of the Godhead depicted in any form — no Father-figure,
no Christ portrait, no dove-as-portrait; symbols and scenery carry the
doctrine.

## Do-Not Checklist (curation gate)

- NO Person of the Godhead in any form (symbolic substitutes: open Bible over
  a plain cross, unconsumed burning bush, anchor, crown of thorns on an empty
  cross, lamplight on the Book, covenant rainbow).
- NO halos, NO crucifixes with corpus, NO baroque/iconographic conventions
  that read as "Catholic art".
- NO color beyond black/cream/grey + one warm wash — maximalism is the
  explicit anti-type (`reformed/deities/yhwh.md`).
- NO satirical/polemical anti-Catholic imagery (the historic woodcut
  tradition is full of it; the project's ecumenical guardrail forbids it).
- NO readable text; woodcut lettering stays abstract pen-strokes.
- Human witnesses appear only inside narrative scenes, never as frontal
  devotional portraits. Would a thoughtful Reformed believer read this as
  Reformation-austere rather than merely "unfinished"?

## Calibration Decisions

- DEDUCTION §5.3(3a): saints/biblical witnesses render as **narrative
  woodcut scenes** (figures within an event, camera at distance) per entry,
  not symbolic still-lifes — the witness entries narrate acts (Moses before
  the bush, David's psalm, Paul's shipwreck), and the Geneva-Bible woodcut
  grammar is narrative; symbolic still-lifes are reserved for the deity,
  cosmology, and abstract entries. The "no divine persons, symbols only"
  reading ships as written in DEDUCTION §3.4.
