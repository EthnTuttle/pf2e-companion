# Style block — pentecostal (prophetic-vision acrylic)

Register (grounded in `pentecostal/pack.toml`: "active spiritual-warfare
cosmology in the Frank Peretti This Present Darkness key" and
`pentecostal/classes/oracle.md`'s "charismatic without health-and-wealth
triumphalism"): the modern prophetic/worship-art genre — large acrylic or
digital painting, saturated color over darkness, visible energetic brushwork,
light as the main actor. This is the one lens where full emotional saturation
is *correct*.

Palette (named colors): midnight indigo ground lit by flame orange,
gold-white glory-light, dove white, emerald oasis, crimson; never pastel.

## Prefix

Contemporary prophetic-vision painting, acrylic/digital with bold expressive
brushwork, midnight-indigo darkness pierced by flame-orange and gold-white
glory light, worship and spiritual-warfare themes with hope dominant, light
itself as the visible protagonist, no textural kitsch. Never depict God the
Father as a person. Subject:

## Suffix

Reverent devotional-fantasy illustration, dignified, no gore. Absolutely no
text, no letters, no inscriptions, no signage, no watermark, no border, no
frame, no logo. No copyrighted fantasy-franchise visual references. No real
identifiable living or recent persons. Never depict God the Father as a
person; the Spirit appears as fire, wind, or the dove.

## Do-Not Checklist (curation gate)

- NO depiction of God the Father as a person; Spirit as fire / wind / dove
  only.
- NO health-and-wealth kitsch: offering buckets, private jets, stage smoke,
  merchandise tables, megachurch interiors, brand signage.
- NO demonic-gore horror; warfare imagery stays light-vs-dark with hope
  dominant — angels win.
- NO mocking ecstatic practice (raised hands, tongues, falling under the
  Spirit are rendered tenderly, in silhouette toward light).
- NO readable text or denominational signage.
- Would a thoughtful Pentecostal recognize this as affectionate rather than
  kitsch?

## Calibration Decisions

- DEDUCTION §5.3(3d): dress and interiors are **present-day modest
  contemporary** — the lens's tradition is a living present-tense movement
  and timeless-robes would read as costume drama against its own register.
  Guardrails still bind: no stage/arena production look, no brands, faces in
  worship stay small and turned toward the light.
