# Style block — orthodox (Byzantine icon)

Register (grounded in `orthodox/pack.toml`: "late-Byzantine visual register
(Hagia Sophia, gold leaf, royal porphry)" and `orthodox/saints/gregory-palamas.md`'s
Tabor light as "physical, perceptible, but uncreated"): the icon — egg tempera
on gold-ground panel, flat inverse perspective, no cast shadows, no
naturalistic depth.

CRITICAL SENSITIVITY: icons are liturgical objects, not decoration. Never ship
pastiche "icons" that parody veneration. The image reads as *an icon
photographed respectfully*: straight-on, full gold ground, no cropping of
faces. Where the app context is playful (a token), prefer the
architectural-vista or symbol variant.

Palette (named colors): burnished gold ground, porphyry purple-red,
lapis/olive drapery, white highlights as uncreated-light assiste, dark
cypress green.

## Suffix

Reverent devotional-fantasy illustration, dignified, no gore. Absolutely no
text, no letters, no inscriptions (iconographic name-titles must remain
abstract glyph-marks only), no watermark, no border, no frame, no logo.
No copyrighted fantasy-franchise visual references. No real identifiable
living or recent persons. God the Father is NEVER depicted (only the
incarnate Son may be imaged); the Spirit appears only as a dove at
Theophany-type scenes.

## Do-Not Checklist (curation gate)

- NO depiction of God the Father — absolute (Great Moscow Synod 1663);
  NO Holy Spirit except as the dove in a Theophany-type scene.
- NO 3D naturalistic shading, cast shadows, motion blur, narrative comic
  dynamism, gore (crucifixion imagery stays stylized).
- NO readable text/letters/inscriptions; halo or titulus marks stay abstract.
- NO cropping of faces, NO off-center "artsy" angles on the panel itself;
  straight-on, full gold ground.
- Christ incarnate and the Theotokos may be depicted; hands in traditional
  gesture; frontal half-length.
- Would a thoughtful Orthodox believer recognize this as an icon treated with
  liturgical respect rather than decoration?

## Calibration Decisions

- DEDUCTION §5.3(3b): **straight-on icon panels for portrait/hero slots**;
  icon-in-architecture (apse wall / iconostasis context) reserved for vistas
  and playful small contexts (tokens). Rationale: the frontal hieratic
  register IS the tradition's visual grammar — presenting the panel straight
  preserves venerational dignity; contextual architecture frames it where the
  app is casual, avoiding "icon as wallpaper".
